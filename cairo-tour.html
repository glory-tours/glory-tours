A DAY IN HISTORIC CAIRO

Cairo Historical Tour

Discover three memorable sides of Cairo — grand Islamic architecture, Coptic heritage and the lively atmosphere of a traditional bazaar.

Your Journey, Step by Step

The suggested route starts at the Citadel of Mohamed Ali, continues to the Hanging Church in Old Cairo, and ends among the lanes and shops of Khan el-Khalili. Visit order and access may be adjusted to opening hours and local conditions.

01 / THE CITADEL

Citadel of Mohamed Ali

Begin at the historic Citadel of Saladin and admire the Mosque of Muhammad Ali, its elegant Ottoman-style domes and sweeping views over Cairo. Enjoy time to explore the landmark and take photographs.

02 / COPTIC CAIRO

The Hanging Church

Continue to Old Cairo and discover one of Egypt's best-known historic churches. Explore its distinctive architecture, peaceful atmosphere and the surrounding heritage area.

03 / OLD MARKET

Khan el-Khalili

End the day in the vibrant lanes of Khan el-Khalili. Browse traditional shops, discover local crafts and souvenirs, and enjoy the atmosphere of one of Cairo's most famous markets.

Free time for shopping and photos.
